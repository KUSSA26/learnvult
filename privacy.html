LearnVult Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

LearnVult is an app for secondary schools. Teachers share learning materials; students study them online, offline, and by sharing with classmates nearby. Most students are under 18, so we collect as little as we can and never use anyone's information for advertising.

In short: we keep your account details, the materials you upload, and simple records of what you download and share, so the app works and schools can see how it is used. We do not sell anything, show ads, track your location, or use advertising or analytics tools.

1. What we keep

  • Your account: name, username, email address, role (student, teacher or administrator), school, and your class or the subjects you teach.
  • Joining a school: anything you add to your verification request, and your school administrator's decision. For a school registration: the school's details and the contact details given.
  • Materials you upload: the files and their details (title, subject, class, topic, description). Your name is shown with them.
  • Activity: when you last signed in; that you downloaded a material (one record per material and version); that you shared or received a material nearby (the material, whether you sent or received it, and when, never who the other person was).
  • Reports you make about a material. Moderators see the report but not who made it.
  • Notifications about your account, your uploads and your reports, and announcements for your school or class.
  • Your folders, favourites and "saved for later" list, so they appear on all your phones.
  • A push notification token for your phone, only while push notifications are on.
  • Administrative records: a log of actions taken by administrators, moderators and teachers (for example approving an account or a material), including who did it.
  • Sign-in protection: the username used in failed sign-in attempts, kept for up to a day so repeated guessing can be blocked.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos (other than files you choose to upload), marks, test results or browsing history.

2. What stays on your phone

Your sign-in is kept in the phone's secure storage. Downloaded files, library details, folders, favourites and the list of materials you recently opened are stored privately for LearnVult. They are deleted from the phone when you sign out.

3. How we use it

  • To check that you belong to the school you chose, and show you the materials meant for your school, class and subjects.
  • To keep your materials, folders and favourites in step across your phones, including after you have been offline.
  • To review uploads and reports, and to keep LearnVult safe and suitable for students.
  • To tell you about decisions on your account and uploads, and about announcements.
  • To give schools and LearnVult administrators totals (for example how many downloads a subject had). Statistics never list individual students.

Your information is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to build profiles of you.

4. Who can see it

  • You can see your own profile.
  • Your school's administrators can see the profiles of their school's members (name, username, role, class, account status and last sign-in), but not email addresses. They review verification requests, uploads and reports for their school.
  • LearnVult platform administrators can see all profiles and records, to run and protect the service.
  • Other users see only what is needed: an uploader's name on their materials, an author's name on announcements, and, during nearby sharing, your first name and role.

We use these services to run LearnVult. Each receives only what it needs:

  • Supabase stores the database, uploaded files and sign-in details, on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (European Union).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications. It receives your phone's push token and the notification text.
  • Google (Gmail) sends account emails such as sign-up confirmations and password resets. It receives your email address and the email.
  • GitHub hosts the app downloads. The app asks GitHub for the newest version about twice a day; like any website, GitHub can see the request's internet address.

5. Nearby sharing

Files you share nearby go directly from phone to phone and do not pass through our servers. Nearby phones see only your first name and role. Android asks for location and nearby-devices permission so phones can find each other; LearnVult does not record your location. Each share is counted later (which material, sent or received, and when) without recording the other person.

6. Children and schools

Students join LearnVult through their school, and a school administrator checks each account before it is activated. We ask each school to inform parents or guardians, and to obtain any consent its rules or the law require, before students sign up. Students may ask a parent, guardian or teacher for help with any privacy question. LearnVult contains no ads and no advertising or analytics tools.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account and its records are kept while your account exists.
  • Notifications are kept for 90 days.
  • When your account is deleted, your profile, notifications, folders, favourites, saved list, push tokens and download and share records are deleted. Reports you made remain, without your name. Materials you uploaded for your school may be kept or reassigned by your school. Administrative records cannot be changed or deleted and keep the name of whoever took each action, so that decisions stay accountable.

8. Security

All connections use encryption (HTTPS/TLS). Every request is checked by the server, so people can only see and change what their role allows. Passwords are never stored in readable form, and repeated wrong passwords temporarily block sign-in. On shared phones, signing out removes your data from the phone and stops your notifications reaching it.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Change your name in Settings.
  • Turn push notifications off in Settings → Push notifications or in your phone's settings.
  • Ask for your account to be deleted in Settings → Delete my account. Administrators process the request.
  • To see, correct or delete your information, or for any question, email us (below).

10. Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date above, and tell users in the app when the change matters to them.
