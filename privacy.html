LearnVult Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

LearnVult is an app for secondary schools. Teachers share learning materials; students study them online, offline, and by sharing with classmates nearby. Most students are under 18, so we collect as little as we can and never use anyone's information for advertising.

In short: we keep your account details, the materials you upload, your quiz results, and simple records of what you download and share, so the app works and schools can see how it is used. We do not sell anything, show ads, track your location, or use advertising or analytics tools.

1. What we keep

  • Your account: name, username, email address, role (student, teacher, General user or administrator), school, and your class or the subjects you teach. General users belong to no school.
  • Joining a school: anything you add to your verification request, and your school administrator's decision. For a school registration: the school's details and the contact details given.
  • Materials you upload: the files and their details (title, subject, class, topic, description). Your name is shown with them.
  • Activity: when you last signed in; that you downloaded a material (one record per material and version); that you shared or received a material nearby (the material, whether you sent or received it, and when, never who the other person was).
  • Quizzes: for each quiz you finish, your answers, score, the time it took, the day it was finished (in your time zone, for streaks) and whether you were offline. From these we work out your streaks, badges, leaderboard points, experience points (XP) and level. The daily challenge and "Review my mistakes" sessions are kept the same way. We also keep whether you chose to leave the leaderboards. Quizzes teachers write, and questions reported as wrong (without your name).
  • Class wins: when a student earns a badge or reaches a new level, a short record of it for their class, and the stickers classmates send (which sticker, and who sent it).
  • "Helpful" marks: which materials you marked as helpful, one mark per material. Materials show only the total.
  • Exam dates added by LearnVult or your school, with who added them.
  • Your notes and highlights in materials: the words you highlighted, what you wrote, the material and page. Only you can see them; we keep them so they appear on all your phones.
  • Glossary terms added by LearnVult administrators, with who added them.
  • Homework a teacher sets for a class: the quiz or material, the due time and who set it. Whether you have done it is worked out from your quiz results; nothing more is recorded.
  • Material requests: the subject, class level and short topic you ask for, who asked, and who said "Me too", so you can be told when a teacher answers. Requests close by themselves after 90 days.
  • Voice notes teachers record on their materials and quiz questions (up to 2 minutes), with the teacher's name.
  • Seasonal events set up by LearnVult, with who set them up, and the event badges you earn.
  • Shared questions: whether a teacher shares their quiz questions with other teachers, and questions LearnVult hid from the shared bank, with who hid them.
  • Games played together: when you play a duel or group study game, your answers and score are kept like any quiz result. Games count for your streak and XP, never for leaderboard points, the school league, homework or certificates.
  • The school league: each month's league quizzes, chosen by LearnVult administrators (with who chose them), and each month's final board: for every school, its rank, score, how many students took part and how many active students it has.
  • Your avatar: the colour, picture and frame you choose, all drawn by the app. Avatars never use photos.
  • AI quiz writing: when a teacher or LearnVult asks AI to write a quiz, a record of who asked, when, from which material, and whether it worked (used for the daily limits).
  • Reports you make about a material. Moderators see the report but not who made it.
  • Notifications about your account, your uploads and your reports, and announcements for your school or class.
  • Your folders, favourites and "saved for later" list, so they appear on all your phones.
  • A push notification token for your phone, only while push notifications are on.
  • Administrative records: a log of actions taken by administrators, moderators and teachers (for example approving an account or a material), including who did it.
  • Sign-in protection: the username used in failed sign-in attempts, kept for up to a day so repeated guessing can be blocked.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos (other than files you choose to upload), school marks or exam results, or browsing history. LearnVult quiz results are kept only as described above.

2. What stays on your phone

Your sign-in is kept in the phone's secure storage. Downloaded files, library details, folders, favourites, the list of materials you recently opened, downloaded quizzes (with their answers, so they can be marked offline), your quiz results and quizzes in progress are stored privately for LearnVult. They are deleted from the phone when you sign out; your quiz history comes back when you sign in again. The streak reminder is set on the phone itself.

Some things are kept only on your phone and never sent to us: your study goals, study timer sessions, your timetable and its reminders, and the low-data setting. Read aloud uses your phone's own voice; the text is not sent anywhere. Teachers' printable quizzes are made on the phone. Also only on your phone: where you stopped reading each material, the search index of your downloaded files (so you can search inside them offline), your flashcard marks, and your maths practice and word game results. Certificates are made as a PDF on your phone from your synced quiz results and badges; they are not sent to us. Homework reminders are set on the phone itself. Your collectible cards are worked out on your phone from your quiz history.

3. How we use it

  • To check that you belong to the school you chose, and show you the materials meant for your school, class and subjects.
  • To keep your materials, folders and favourites in step across your phones, including after you have been offline.
  • To review uploads and reports, and to keep LearnVult safe and suitable for students.
  • To tell you about decisions on your account and uploads, new quizzes for your class, and announcements.
  • To mark quizzes, and to work out streaks, badges, levels, leaderboards and the most improved board, and to show class wins.
  • To show homework and its due time, tell students when homework is set, and tell people who asked for a material when a teacher answers.
  • To rank schools in the monthly league, from their students' first try at each league quiz, without ever listing students.
  • To give schools and LearnVult administrators totals (for example how many downloads a subject had). These statistics never list individual students. A teacher sees totals for the quizzes they wrote, and, for quizzes set for one of their classes, each of that class's students' results. Teachers see totals of how far their own materials and quizzes reached. School administrators and LearnVult can export the period's totals by subject, class level and class (downloads, quizzes taken, average score and the number of active students), never names.

Your information is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to build profiles of you.

4. Who can see it

  • You can see your own profile.
  • Your school's administrators can see the profiles of their school's members (name, username, role, class, account status and last sign-in), but not email addresses. They review verification requests, uploads and reports for their school.
  • LearnVult platform administrators can see all profiles and records, to run and protect the service.
  • Your teachers see your results on quizzes they set for your class. For homework, your class's teachers see whether each student has done it and when, and how the class is doing by topic. General users' names are never shown to teachers.
  • The class board ranks classes by the average points of their students, within the school, never naming students. Every student in a class counts towards its average, including those who left the leaderboards, since no one is named.
  • Material requests are seen by students and teachers of your school only, without anyone's name, with how many people said "Me too". Your school's administrators and LearnVult can remove a request.
  • Voice notes are heard by the same people who can see the material or quiz, with the teacher's name. Your school's administrators and LearnVult can delete them.
  • Shared questions are seen by teachers in other schools with the name of the school they came from (or "LearnVult"), never the teacher's name.
  • The school league is seen by everyone signed in to LearnVult: school names, ranks, scores and how many students took part. Students' names are never shown.
  • Your avatar is shown wherever your name is already shown in LearnVult, such as leaderboards, class wins and games played together.
  • Leaderboards show students' first name, last initial and points, only to members of that class or school. You can leave the leaderboards in Settings (you still see your own rank), and your school or LearnVult can hide anyone from them. General users do not appear on leaderboards. The most improved board follows the same rules.
  • Class wins show your first name, last initial and the badge or level to your classmates for 14 days. On your own wins you see how many of each sticker you got, not who sent them. Students who left the leaderboards, or were hidden from them, have no class wins.
  • Other users see only what is needed: an uploader's name on their materials, an author's name on announcements and quizzes, and, during nearby sharing, your first name and role. Materials and quizzes published by LearnVult show "LearnVult", not the administrator's name.

We use these services to run LearnVult. Each receives only what it needs:

  • Supabase stores the database, uploaded files and sign-in details, on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (European Union).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications. It receives your phone's push token and the notification text.
  • Google (Gmail) sends account emails such as sign-up confirmations and password resets. It receives your email address and the email.
  • An AI service (Google Gemini at present; it may be changed to Groq or Anthropic) writes draft quizzes when a teacher or LearnVult asks for one. It receives only the title and text of the teaching material, never anyone's name or account details. Teachers check every question before it is published.
  • GitHub hosts the app downloads. The app asks GitHub for the newest version about twice a day; like any website, GitHub can see the request's internet address.

5. Nearby sharing and games

Files you share nearby go directly from phone to phone and do not pass through our servers. Nearby phones see only your first name and role. Android asks for location and nearby-devices permission so phones can find each other; LearnVult does not record your location. Each share is counted later (which material, sent or received, and when) without recording the other person.

Games played together (duels and group study) also go directly from phone to phone, without internet. Players see each other's first name and avatar. To check that a quiz meant for one school or class is only played within it, your phone tells the host's phone the codes of your school and class (not their names). The host's phone marks the answers and sees each player's score; at the end of group study it shows the top three by first name, and students who left the leaderboards are shown as "A player". Your game result is sent to us later, like any quiz result.

6. Children and schools

Students join LearnVult through their school, and a school administrator checks each account before it is activated. We ask each school to inform parents or guardians, and to obtain any consent its rules or the law require, before students sign up. Students may ask a parent, guardian or teacher for help with any privacy question. LearnVult contains no ads and no advertising or analytics tools.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account and its records are kept while your account exists.
  • Notifications are kept for 90 days.
  • Each month's final school league board is kept for 12 months.
  • Material requests close after 90 days; answered requests stay on the board for 30 days. When a voice note is deleted, its recording is removed from storage within about an hour.
  • Your quiz history is kept while your account exists, including if you leave your school and become a General user.
  • When a material is deleted, its files are removed from storage within about an hour; a record that it existed is kept for moderation.
  • When your account is deleted, your profile, notifications, folders, favourites, saved list, push tokens, download and share records, quiz results, streaks and badges, class wins, stickers you sent, "helpful" marks, notes and highlights, material requests and "Me too" marks, and homework you set are deleted. Voice notes you recorded stay with their material or quiz, under your name, and can be deleted by your school. Reports you made remain, without your name. Materials you uploaded for your school may be kept or reassigned by your school. Administrative records cannot be changed or deleted and keep the name of whoever took each action, so that decisions stay accountable.

8. Security

All connections use encryption (HTTPS/TLS). Every request is checked by the server, so people can only see and change what their role allows. Passwords are never stored in readable form, and repeated wrong passwords temporarily block sign-in. On shared phones, signing out removes your data from the phone and stops your notifications reaching it.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Change your name in Settings.
  • Turn push notifications off in Settings → Push notifications or in your phone's settings, and the streak reminder off in Settings → Quizzes.
  • Leave the quiz leaderboards in Settings → Quizzes. This also stops your class wins being shown.
  • Ask for your account to be deleted in Settings → Delete my account. Administrators process the request.
  • To see, correct or delete your information, or for any question, email us (below).
